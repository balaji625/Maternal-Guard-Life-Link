## 📌 Problem Statement

Postpartum hemorrhage remains one of the leading causes of maternal mortality, especially in rural healthcare systems where:

- Risk assessment is reactive instead of predictive  
- Emergency donor identification is delayed  
- Communication between hospitals and institutes is fragmented  
- Critical golden-hour response time is often lost  

Traditional systems respond after danger.  
**Maternal-Guard predicts before it happens.**

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Maternal-Guard & Life-Link is a modular AI-powered healthcare web platform consisting of two intelligent engines:

---

### 🧠 Component A — AI Risk Prediction Engine

- XGBoost Multi-Class Classifier  
- Real-Time Risk Prediction  
- Confidence Scoring  
- Explainable AI (Feature Importance Visualization)  
- REST API-based Inference  
- Automated Clinical Email Alerts  

The system provides doctors not only with a risk category but also the top contributing clinical features.

---

### ❤️ Component B — Life-Link Emergency Donor Network

- Blood Group Compatibility Filtering  
- Hemoglobin Validation (>12.5 g/dL)  
- District-Based Filtering  
- One-Tap Emergency SOS Trigger  
- GPS-Based Donor Location Redirection  
- Privacy Masking Until Admin Authorization  

From prediction to donor match — in seconds.

## 🧠 Machine Learning Details

**Dataset Used:**  
Maternal Health Risk Dataset  

**Preprocessing Steps:**
- Label Encoding  
- Feature Selection  
- Outlier Handling  
- 80/20 Train-Test Split  

**Model Used:**  
XGBoost Classifier  

**Prediction Outputs:**
- Risk Level (Low / Mid / High)  
- AI Confidence Score  
- Feature Importance Ranking  
- Top Contributing Clinical Feature  

The model ensures transparency through explainability rather than black-box predictions.

## ⚡ Emergency Workflow (Under 10 Seconds)

1. Patient vitals entered  
2. AI risk prediction generated  
3. High-risk threshold detected  
4. Compatible donors filtered instantly  
5. GPS redirect initiated  
6. Automated clinical alert email sent  

This ensures rapid response during postpartum hemorrhage scenarios.
